# Reflector

You can find a video tutorial for Reflectors [▶ here](https://youtu.be/U3b55PFMXsc?list=PLAVxVgoBmZhM).

It allows reflection to be performed according to the incoming Effect value sign and redirects the final calculated Effect value to one or more Actors based on the configuration defined by the resolved **Reflector Stat** and its Signature.

Reflection is evaluated separately for the **Source Actor** and the **Target Actor**.

The Reflector Stat is resolved as follows:

- For the **Target Actor**, the Reflector Stat is obtained directly from the **Target Stat's Reflector Relative Stat**.
- For the **Source Actor**, the Reflector Stat is obtained from the **Source Actor** using the **Stat Key** referenced by the **Target Stat's Reflector Relative Key**.

The resolved Stat must be a **Reflector Stat**.

## Variations

Ships as `BS`, `AS` and `MAS` × `Float` `Int`, in five container variations — thirty assets in all. See the [Processor naming scheme](/docs/Assets/Processor/ProcessorVariations).

| Suffix | Expects | Use case |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `_Normal` | A plain Reflector Stat | *Thorns* — reflect a share of any incoming damage back at whoever dealt it. |
| `_SrcSep` | A Source Separated Stat | *Curse of Reprisal* — reflect only the damage dealt by the boss that applied the curse. |
| `_SigSep` | A Signature Separated Stat | *Flame Mirror* — bounce fire damage back; let physical damage through untouched. |
| `_SigNSrcSep` | Signature **and** Source Separated | Reflect one enemy's fire damage, and nothing else. |
| `_Effector` | Resolved on the **Effector's Actor** | A spiked shield reflects whatever it blocks, independent of the wielder's own Stats. |

The Precontribution form reflects the **incoming** value, before Modifiers and crits are applied. Use the [Postcontribution Reflector](/docs/Assets/Processor/Processors/Standard/Postcontribution/Reflector) to reflect the final number instead.
