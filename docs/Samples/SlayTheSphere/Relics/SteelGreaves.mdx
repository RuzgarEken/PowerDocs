# Steel Greaves

Whenever an Attack would deal **4 or less** [unblocked damage](/docs/Samples/SlayTheSphere/Keywords#unblocked-damage), increase it to **5** instead.

## Implementation

This Effect raises the floor on the unblocked Attack damage the player deals:

<EffectExample
  name="Effect_GivenAttackDamageLImiterMin_STS"
  targetStat="GivenAttackDamageMin"
  valueStat="GivenAttackDamageMin"
  value={5}
  duration="1 turn"
/>

Any hit of `4` or less is clamped up to `5` before it lands. The minimum comes from <PowerRef>Stat_GivenAttackDamageMin_5_STS</PowerRef>.
