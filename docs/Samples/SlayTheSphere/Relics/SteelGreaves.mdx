# Steel Greaves

Whenever an Attack would deal **4 or less** [unblocked damage](/docs/Samples/SlayTheSphere/Keywords#unblocked-damage), increase it to **5** instead.

## Implementation

This Effect raises the floor on the unblocked Attack damage the player deals:

<div className="prp-packs">

<details>
<summary><strong>ApplyingAttackDamageLimiterMin</strong> — Effect</summary>

<EffectExample
  name="ApplyingAttackDamageLimiterMin"
  targetStat="ApplyingAttackDamageMin"
  valueStat="ApplyingAttackDamageMin"
  value={5}
  duration="1 turn"
/>

</details>

</div>

Any hit of `4` or less is clamped up to `5` before it lands. The minimum comes from <PowerRef>Stat_ApplyingAttackDamageMin_5_STS</PowerRef>.
