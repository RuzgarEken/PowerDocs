# Relics

Relics are passive items. Each one runs a Skill that hooks into combat events and stays active for the whole run.

Like Cards, each Relic composes a small Repository of Stats and drives a handful of Effects through its own Skill &mdash; the Skill just runs once on pickup instead of on every play.

## Ancient Barrier

- Prevent the **first instance of HP loss** each combat.

## Cursed Wraps

- Whenever you play a **Power** card, a random card in your hand costs **0 [Energy](/docs/Samples/SlayTheSphere/Keywords#energy)** for that turn.

## Divine Cask

- Doubles the effectiveness of Potions.

## Iron Bracers

- At the start of your turn, lose **15 [Block](/docs/Samples/SlayTheSphere/Keywords#block)** instead of losing all Block.

## Raider's Trophy

- Whenever an enemy dies, gain **1 Energy** and [draw](/docs/Samples/SlayTheSphere/Keywords#draw-pile) **1 card**.

## Spice Root

- You can no longer become [**Weak**](/docs/Samples/SlayTheSphere/Keywords#weak).

## Steel Greaves

- Whenever an Attack would deal **4 or less** [unblocked damage](/docs/Samples/SlayTheSphere/Keywords#unblocked-damage), increase it to **5** instead.
