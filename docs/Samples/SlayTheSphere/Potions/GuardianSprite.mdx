# Guardian Sprite

When you would die, heal to **30% of your [Max HP](/docs/Samples/SlayTheSphere/Keywords#hp)** instead and consume this Potion.

## Implementation

While carried, an `OnMount` pack overrides the player's die condition:

<EffectExample name="Effect_DieImmune" targetStat="DieImmune" value={1} />

When the player would otherwise die, the potion's `ToTarget` pack fires instead:

<PackExample name="Pack_GuardianSprite" target="Self">
  <EffectExample name="Effect_Heal" targetStat="Health" value="30% of Max HP" />
  <EffectExample name="Effect_DecreaseUseBlocker" targetStat="UseBlocker" value={-1} />
</PackExample>

`Effect_DecreaseUseBlocker` consumes the potion so it can't trigger again.
