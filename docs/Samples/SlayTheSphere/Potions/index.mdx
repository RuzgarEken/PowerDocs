# Potions

Potions are one-use consumables. Playing a potion runs its Skill once and then removes it from your belt.

Potions also compose a Repository of Stats like Cards and Relics do, but their Skill only ever runs once, since the potion is consumed on use.

## Blast Potion

- Deal **12** damage to **everyone**, including yourself and all enemies.

## Guardian Sprite

- When you would die, heal to **30% of your [Max HP](/docs/Samples/SlayTheSphere/Keywords#hp)** instead and consume this Potion.

## Heal Potion

- Restore **20% of your Max HP**.
