# Void Assassin

During your next turn, your **Attack** cards deal [**double damage**](/docs/Samples/SlayTheSphere/Keywords#double-damage).

Void Assassin doesn't modify damage directly — it raises a persistent <PowerRef>Stat_VoidAssassin_1_STS</PowerRef> trigger. A shared converter pack listens for the next Attack card played during your next turn:

<div className="prp-packs">

<details>
<summary><strong>VoidAssassinToDoubleDamageConverter</strong> — Effect Pack</summary>

<PackExample name="VoidAssassinToDoubleDamageConverter" target="Next Attack card">
  <EffectExample name="DoubleDamage" targetStat="DamageMultiplier" value={1} duration="1 use" />
  <EffectExample name="ResetVoidAssassin" targetStat="VoidAssassin" value={0} />
</PackExample>

</details>

</div>

`ResetVoidAssassin` clears the trigger straight after, so only that one attack is doubled.
