# Void Assassin

During your next turn, your **Attack** cards deal [**double damage**](/docs/Samples/SlayTheSphere/Keywords#double-damage).

## Implementation

Void Assassin doesn't modify damage directly — it raises a persistent <PowerRef>Stat_VoidAssassin_1_STS</PowerRef> trigger. A shared converter pack listens for the next Attack card played during your next turn:

<PackExample name="Pack_VoidAssassinToDoubleDamageConverter_STS" target="Next Attack card">
  <EffectExample name="Effect_DoubleDamage" targetStat="DamageMultiplier" value={1} duration="1 use" />
  <EffectExample name="Effect_ResetVoidAssassin" targetStat="VoidAssassin" value={0} />
</PackExample>

`Effect_ResetVoidAssassin` clears the trigger straight after, so only that one attack is doubled.
