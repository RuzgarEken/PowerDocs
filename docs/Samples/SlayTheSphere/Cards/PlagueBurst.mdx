# Plague Burst

Apply **6 [Poison](/docs/Samples/SlayTheSphere/Keywords#poison)**.

When the affected enemy dies, deal damage equal to its **[Max HP](/docs/Samples/SlayTheSphere/Keywords#hp)** to **all enemies**.
