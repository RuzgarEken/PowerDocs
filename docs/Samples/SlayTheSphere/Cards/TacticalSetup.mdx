# Tactical Setup

Put a card from your hand on top of your [draw pile](/docs/Samples/SlayTheSphere/Keywords#draw-pile).

It costs **0 [Energy](/docs/Samples/SlayTheSphere/Keywords#energy)** until played.

The card places the chosen hand card on top of the draw pile, then attaches a cost modifier to it:

A separate <PowerRef>Pack_RemoveZeroManaModifierOnUse</PowerRef> watches for that specific card being played and removes both the cost modifier and itself, so the discount applies only once.
