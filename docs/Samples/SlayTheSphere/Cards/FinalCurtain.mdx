# Final Curtain

Can only be played if your [draw pile](/docs/Samples/SlayTheSphere/Keywords#draw-pile) is empty.

Deal **50** damage to **all enemies**.

The "draw pile must be empty" rule is a use-block injector. It reads <PowerRef>Key_DrawPileCardCount</PowerRef> and adds a UseBlocker to the card whenever the draw pile is **not** empty, so Final Curtain only becomes playable once the pile is exhausted:

/>

</details>

</div>
