# Cards

Cards are the core of the demo. Every card references a Skill that runs when the card is played, and the three card types differ in how that Skill behaves.

## How cards are built

Every card's Skill points at a small **Repository** of Stats composed from a shared library in `Stats/Cards/General`. Generic Keys such as **Damage**, **EnergyCost**, [**Block**](/docs/Samples/SlayTheSphere/Keywords#block), or **CardType** are filled in with reusable, pre-named value Stats (`Stat_Damage_6`, `Stat_EnergyCost_1`, `Stat_CardType_Attack`, and so on). A small set of generic Effects &mdash; `Effect_AttackDamage_STS`, `Effect_Block_STS`, `Effect_EnergyCost_STS`, etc. &mdash; read those Keys dynamically and apply them, so the same Effect is reused by every card of that kind; only the composed Stats change per card. Cards that need something the shared library can't express add their own Effects and Stats on top, described in that card's **Implementation** section below.

## Attack

Attack cards deal damage to enemies.

### Bloodied Shiv

- Costs **1 additional [Energy](/docs/Samples/SlayTheSphere/Keywords#energy)** for each time you have lost HP during the current combat.
- Deal **12** damage.

### Disarm

- Deal **3** damage.
- Apply **1 [Weak](/docs/Samples/SlayTheSphere/Keywords#weak)**.

### Final Curtain

- Can only be played if your [draw pile](/docs/Samples/SlayTheSphere/Keywords#draw-pile) is empty.
- Deal **50** damage to **all enemies**.

### Shred

- Costs **1 less Energy** for each card [discarded](/docs/Samples/SlayTheSphere/Keywords#discard-pile) this turn.
- Deal **7** damage **3 times**.

### Strike

- Deal **6** damage.

### Venom Burst

- Deal **7** damage.
- If the target is [**Poisoned**](/docs/Samples/SlayTheSphere/Keywords#poison), deal **7** damage again.

## Skill

Skill cards provide utility effects instead of direct damage.

### Defend

- Gain **5 Block**.

### Expose Weakness

- Apply **99 [Vulnerable](/docs/Samples/SlayTheSphere/Keywords#vulnerable)**.
- [**Exhaust.**](/docs/Samples/SlayTheSphere/Keywords#exhaust)

### Focus

- Discard **3** cards.
- Gain **2 Energy**.

### Fortified Guard

- Gain **5 Block**.
- Block is **not removed** at the start of your next turn.

### Plague Burst

- Apply **6 Poison**.
- When the affected enemy dies, deal damage equal to its **[Max HP](/docs/Samples/SlayTheSphere/Keywords#hp)** to **all enemies**.

### Quick Hands

- Draw **3** cards.
- Discard **1** card.

### Shadow Double

- At the start of your next turn, draw **X** cards and gain **X Energy**.
- **Exhaust.**

### Tactical Setup

- Put a card from your hand on top of your draw pile.
- It costs **0 Energy** until played.

### Void Assassin

- During your next turn, your **Attack** cards deal [**double damage**](/docs/Samples/SlayTheSphere/Keywords#double-damage).

## Power

Power cards apply persistent effects that remain active for the rest of combat unless explicitly removed.

### Barbed Armor

- Whenever you are attacked, deal **3** damage back to the attacker.

### Blade Storm

- Whenever you play a card, deal **1** damage to **all enemies**.

### Phantom Veil

- Gain **2 [Intangible](/docs/Samples/SlayTheSphere/Keywords#intangible)**.
- At the end of your turn, lose **1 [Dexterity](/docs/Samples/SlayTheSphere/Keywords#dexterity)**.

### Prepared Scheme

- At the end of your turn, [**retain up to 1 card**](/docs/Samples/SlayTheSphere/Keywords#retain).

### Toxic Edge

- Whenever an **Attack** deals [unblocked damage](/docs/Samples/SlayTheSphere/Keywords#unblocked-damage), apply **1 Poison**.
