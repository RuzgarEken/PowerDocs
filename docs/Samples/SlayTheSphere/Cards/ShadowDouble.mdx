# Shadow Double

At the start of your next turn, [draw](/docs/Samples/SlayTheSphere/Keywords#draw-pile) **X** cards and gain **X [Energy](/docs/Samples/SlayTheSphere/Keywords#energy)**.

[**Exhaust.**](/docs/Samples/SlayTheSphere/Keywords#exhaust)

Instead of a fixed cost, Shadow Double composes <PowerRef>Stat_DynamicEnergyRequirement_1_STS</PowerRef> so its cost is resolved live. Its draw and Energy Effects are scheduled against the **NextTurn** Stat containers rather than the current turn, so they only resolve when your next turn begins:
