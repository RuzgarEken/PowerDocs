**Thorn Shield** is a toggle Skill. While it is toggled on, `WhileToggleOn` applies four Effects: `ActivateAim`, `MoveBlock` (the player cannot move while blocking), `Shield` (raises the **Shielded** flag), and `Parry` (raises the **Parry** flag for a short `0.4` second window at the start of the block).

### Blocking and parry

While **Shielded**, the **ThornShield_ConditionalAttackDamageModifier** Stat is injected into incoming attack damage to reduce it. It is signature-separated, so each attacker is tracked independently.

During the **Parry** window, the **ThornShield_AttackDamageImmune** Stat grants full immunity, negating all damage and emitting an **Immune** result. The parry also drives skill-specific **CritRate** and **CritMultiplier** conditional modifiers so the counter hit lands critically, and applies `ParryDamage` back to the attacker.

### Countering attacks

`BlockMeleeCounterEffects` is an **[Intervener Effect](/docs/Assets/Stat/StatDefinition/Redirector/IntervenerEffect)** list: it reacts to received melee attacks while blocking, before they land, again signature-separated per attacker (relation `IntervenerEffect_ReceivingAS` on the attack's attribute stat).

For ranged attacks, the `BlockRangedCounterEffects` Intervener runs a set of Effects on the blocked projectile: `ReverseDirection` flips its travel vector, `ChangeTeam` switches its team so it now damages enemies, and `ResetHitCount` / `ResetTravelDistance` restore its range — sending the shot back the way it came.

When the Skill is toggled off, `OnToggleOff` syncs the movement and look destination and removes the casting block. A `SkillExtension_BodyPartExtender` script binds the block to a body-part Actor so it originates from the correct bone.
