Holds up to **3 ammo**.

Stuns enemies on hit.

Restores **1 ammo** whenever an enemy is killed.

## Implementation

**Scrap Tossers** is a one-state Skill. While it is cast it locks onto the closest enemy in sight and throws at it; with nobody in sight it throws where you aim. Clicks still move the character during the cast.

While casting, the Skill picks its target and turns toward it. `SetLockOnTarget` writes the closest living enemy in sight into **LockOnTarget** (nobody: empty). A Conditional Effect then locks on when there is a target, and otherwise aims at the pointer. `RotationInputBlock` keeps the facing from changing while you walk. All three are Temporary, so they stop with the cast.

### Ammo

Ammo is the **ScrapTossers_AmmoCount** Stat, clamped to `0`–`3` and defaulting to `3`. On each use, `SpendAmmo` applies `-1`.

To block the Skill while empty, `Add1_AmmoEq0` injects `+1` into the Skill's **UseBlockers** Stat whenever **AmmoCount** equals `0` — the same pattern the cooldown uses. The Skill cannot be used again until ammo is available.

### Attack

`ThorwableObjects_Attack` applies two Effects on hit: `Damage` (a flat `-50` to **Health**) and `Stun`, which adds `1` to the target's **Stun** Stat for `2` seconds.

### Restoring ammo on kill

`KillToAmmoCounterInjector` injects a **CounterEffect** into the enemy's **CounterKillEffects** Stat through `KillToAmmoCounter`, tracking the lethal Effects dealt by this weapon. When an enemy dies, `IncreaseAmmoCount` adds `1` back to **AmmoCount**.
