The **CritRateModifier** Stat adds `1` to **CritRate** as a flat contribution when the player stands within the enemy's ±45° front cone, so the enemy is facing the player: a [Relative Angle](/docs/Assets/Misc/StatCondition/MultiStat/RelativeAngle) condition on the enemy's Position and Direction and the player's Position, **Absolute Angle**, **Lower Or Equal** `45`.
