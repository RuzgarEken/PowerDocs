# Soulpiercer Nail

Can attack horizontally, upward, or downward.

Jumping performs an upward attack, and falling performs a downward attack.

Landing a downward attack on an enemy causes the player to bounce upward.

## Implementation

**Soulpiercer Nail** is a multi-state Skill with three attack directions: **Forward** (State 1), **Upward** (State 2), and **Downward** (State 3).

{/* ![Soulpiercer Nail Skill States](./images/SoulpiercerNail_SkillStates.png) */}

The direction is chosen through **SkillEvolutionNo** ConditionalModifiers. A `StatComparer_IsNotGrounded` check gates the aerial variants: while the player is grounded, the horizontal attack (State 1) is used.

{/* ![Soulpiercer Nail Evolution Dispatch](./images/SoulpiercerNail_EvolutionDispatch.png) */}

While airborne, a dispatcher inspects the Actor's vertical speed. If it is positive, `Effect_EvolutionNoToUpward` adds `1` and the Upward attack (State 2) runs. If it is negative, `Effect_EvolutionNoToDownward` adds `2` and the Downward attack (State 3) runs.

{/* ![Soulpiercer Nail Vertical Speed Comparers](./images/SoulpiercerNail_VerticalSpeedComparers.png) */}

The Downward attack applies its bounce on collision. `Effect_ZeroVerticalSpeed` first cancels the falling velocity, then `Effect_Bounce` sets the Actor's vertical speed to a positive value, launching the player back up. A self `Effect_MoveBlock` briefly locks movement during the strike.

{/* ![Soulpiercer Nail Bounce Effect](./images/SoulpiercerNail_BounceEffect.png) */}

The aerial attacks use their own **VerticalAttackCritRate** Attribute Stat, keeping their critical-hit behavior independent from the horizontal attack.

{/* ![Soulpiercer Nail Vertical Attack Stat](./images/SoulpiercerNail_VerticalAttackStat.png) */}

Like the other 3DCells weapons, the Skill uses the shared cooldown-degenerator and use-blocker mechanism to gate reuse.
