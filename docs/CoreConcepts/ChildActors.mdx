# Child Actors

An Actor can be initialized with a **parent Actor**. Its own Repository stays small — only what belongs to the child — and everything it doesn't have is looked up on the parent.

Typical children are the things an Actor owns or spawns: a weapon, a relic, a potion, a card in hand, a projectile, a body part. In **Slay The Sphere**, every relic, potion and card is a child of the player:

```csharp
Actor.Initialize(item.Repository, source);   // the item's own Stats, with the player as parent
```

The overloads are `Initialize(StatRepositoryModel repository, ActorEntity parentActor)` and `Initialize(ActorEntity parentActor)`.

## What falls back to the parent

| Lookup on the child | If the child doesn't have it |
| --- | --- |
| A Stat whose **Environment** is not in the child's Repository | looked up on the parent |
| A Stat whose Environment exists but the **Container** or the **Stat** is missing | first the Environment's [Parent Environment](/docs/Assets/Stat/Environment) chain, then the parent Actor |
| **Effect Process Definition** not assigned | the parent's, taken when the child initializes |
| An **Actor Extension** the child doesn't have | the parent's extension with that id |

So a weapon only defines its own Stats — its cooldown, its charge, its damage bonus — and still reads the player's `Health`, `Dead` or team through the same keys. Leave a Stat out of the child entirely when it should come from the parent: if the child has its own copy, its own copy wins.

Every lookup on the child goes through these rules, including the **Attribute Stats** of Effects the child sends. A weapon's attack can carry the player's Attack Stat as its Attribute Stat as long as the weapon doesn't define one.

## Things to keep in mind

- **The Source is the child.** An Effect a weapon applies has the weapon as its Source, not the player. Relations resolved on the Source ([Which Actor Resolves a Relation](./RelationResolution)) are looked up on the weapon first and reach the player through the fallback.
- **Reaching the owner on purpose.** To make an Effect target the owner of a child, use the [Parent Actor](/docs/Assets/Misc/Adapter/Value/ParentActor) Value Adapter as the Actor Adapter.
- **Teams.** A child is not a Diplomacy member unless you add it (`PowerWorld.Diplomacy.AddMember`). Relation State and Team Flag targeting only sees members, so a spawned child is not found as an enemy or a teammate on its own.
